## Asking Questions (Patient Only) `[AQ]`

**Definition**：Seek information and clarification” should be related to patient’s medical encounter, not rapport building 

**Guidelines**:
- Counts:
    - “Is this normal?”
    - “Does smoking do that?”
- Does not count: Unrelated to medical encounter “how are your kids?”

## Assertive Responses (Patient Only) `[AR]`

**Definition**: Offering opinions/making requests/introducing new topics/stating preferences/making recommendations/disagreeing, clarifying, or interrupting should be related to the patient’s medical encounter

**Guidelines**:
- Counts: In response to a provider’s question, the patient’s response would only count as assertive if the patient brings up a new topic
- Does not count: If it’s an extension of a response

**Examples**:
- “I feel uncomfortable with [the brace] on”
- “Go ahead and do it”
- “I think we should do another test”
- “I don’t think that is what is really happening”
- “I don’t really want to do that”
- “I looked online and ...” 
- “No” - as long as it is not in response to a provider’s questions
